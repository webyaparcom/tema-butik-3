Size chart

Find your size from your bust measurement

The chart is approximate; a cut can vary by one size. If unsure, try two sizes together at your fitting.

  1. 1Run the tape around the fullest part of the bust, parallel to the floor.
  2. 2Read it without holding your breath or pulling the tape tight.
  3. 3Measure over a thin top; do not add room for a thick jumper.
  4. 4If you fall between two sizes, take the smaller one in relaxed cuts.
